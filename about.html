OUR STORY

Rooted in the Himalayas

We connect Uttarakhand's organic farmers directly with families across India. Every product carries the purity of the mountains and the love of local communities.

Our Journey

A Collective of Mountain Farmers

Founded in 2017, Pahadi Organics started with a simple mission: to preserve Uttarakhand's traditional farming heritage and bring authentic Himalayan produce to modern homes.

Today, we work with hundreds of farmer families, ensuring fair prices, sustainable practices, and chemical-free food.
